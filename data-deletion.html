Data Deletion

To delete all data held by ArtTrong AutoPost or Bunko AutoPost:

  1. Revoke the app's access from the platform itself (for example, Pinterest > Settings > Apps, TikTok > Settings > Manage apps, or Meta > Settings > Apps and websites). Revocation immediately stops all further access.
  2. Email ductrong2601@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my AutoPost data" and the account name concerned. Stored access tokens and private post logs for that account will be deleted within 7 days, and a confirmation email will be sent.

No data is sold or shared with third parties, so there is no third party to notify.
