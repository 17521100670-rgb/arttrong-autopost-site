Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026. Controller: Art Trong (Nguyễn Đức Trọng), Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Contact: ductrong2601@gmail.com.

1. What these tools are

ArtTrong AutoPost and Bunko AutoPost are personal automation tools for a single creator. They post content the creator has already approved, to the creator's own accounts, through the official APIs of Pinterest, TikTok, Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) and Tumblr. They are not offered to the public and they do not collect data from any other person.

2. Data we access

When the creator connects an account with OAuth, the platform may provide: the account identifier and username, the list of the creator's own boards, pins, pages or profiles needed to publish, and the status of a published post. The tools do not ask for, receive or store the creator's platform password. No data about followers, viewers or any other user is collected, profiled or sold.

3. How data is used

Accessed data is used only to (a) publish the creator-approved post to the account the creator selected, (b) confirm whether the post published, and (c) keep a private log of the creator's own posts so duplicates are avoided. Content is labelled as AI-generated where the platform requires it.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with any third party for advertising or any other purpose. Data is sent only to the platform the creator chose, through that platform's official API, to publish the creator's own post.

5. Storage and retention

Access tokens are stored securely in the creator's own credential store and are never published, pasted into chat or committed to any public repository. Post logs are kept only for the creator's own record-keeping and are deleted on request (see Data Deletion).

6. Your rights

The creator may revoke any platform connection at any time from that platform's own settings, which immediately stops further access. Deletion requests can be sent to ductrong2601@gmail.com and will be actioned within 7 days.

7. Children

These tools are not directed at children and do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
